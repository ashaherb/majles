# The developer's words

Owner: **main session**. What the developer said that shapes the product,
quoted exactly, dated, newest last (`.claude/rules/asking.md`). Never summarise,
edit or delete an entry; a later entry can supersede an earlier one by saying
so. A record that rests on the developer's authority links to its entry here,
as `docs/direction.md#d-N`. Anything not here is the team's draft.

Format:

```markdown
## d-N. YYYY-MM-DD: the topic

> "the developer's words, exactly"

Context: what they were answering or reacting to, in one line.
Supersedes: d-M, if it does.
```
